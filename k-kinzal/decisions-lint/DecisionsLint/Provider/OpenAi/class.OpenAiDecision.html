final class OpenAiDecision
    implements Decision

Adapts the OpenAI Decisions API to the Decision interface.

The subject sections are rendered into one text input. Choices become {value, description} entries and score levels become {label, description} entries. Answers are read back by name; a refusal becomes a refusal answer.
