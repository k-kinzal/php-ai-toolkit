final class LayerPage

Renders the symbols of one architecture layer.

Layers come from the project's deptrac configuration, so the page also states which layers this one may depend on. The namespaces the layer spans are listed before its symbols: a layer is usually recognised by the namespaces it owns rather than by the individual classes in it.
