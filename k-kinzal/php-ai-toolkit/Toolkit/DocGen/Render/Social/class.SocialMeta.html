final class SocialMeta

Renders what a link to one page is previewed from elsewhere.

A site of relative links needs no address of its own, but a card shown for a link shared in a chat or on a timeline does: the address is absolute, and so is the image beside it. Nothing is rendered until a project says where its site is published, because a card that names pages and images nobody can fetch is worse than no card at all.
