final class ForbidBroadCatchRule
    implements Rule<Catch_>

Forbids catching Throwable, Exception, and the LogicException and Error families outside a configured boundary layer.

Broad catches swallow programmer errors together with the failures they meant to handle. Only designated boundary files (entry points, middleware, worker loops) may catch these types.
