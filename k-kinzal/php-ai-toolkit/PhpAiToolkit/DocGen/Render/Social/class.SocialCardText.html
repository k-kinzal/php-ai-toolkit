final class SocialCardText

Measures and breaks the lines the social preview card is drawn from.

The card is drawn from what a project happens to be called, so nothing about its text is known in advance: the type is measured against the font it will be drawn with, shrunk until the longest line fits the card, and broken into the lines that are left.
