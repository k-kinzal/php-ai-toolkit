final class LcsMatcher

Matches two sequences by their longest common subsequence.

The shared head and tail of the sequences are matched directly, which is what a typical edit looks like, and only the differing middle is put through the quadratic table. A middle too large for the table is treated as a full replacement, so a diff of two unrelated files stays fast.
