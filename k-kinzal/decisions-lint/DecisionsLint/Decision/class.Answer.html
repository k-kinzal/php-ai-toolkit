classAnswer

packages/decisions-lint/src/Decision/Answer.php:22
final class Answer

The typed answer a decision model gave to one question.

A predicate carries a probability that the statement holds. A choice carries the chosen option, the probability of every option and a confidence. A score carries the probability-weighted level, the probability of every level and a confidence. A refusal carries nothing but the fact that the model declined.
