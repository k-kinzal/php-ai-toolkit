final class CatchThrowVisitor
    extends NodeVisitorAbstract

Collects the throw statements that belong directly to one catch block.

Nested try/catch structures are skipped entirely — their catches are evaluated independently when the rule visits them. Closures, arrow functions, and anonymous classes are skipped because their throws run in another scope.
